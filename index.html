ABOUT US

Phoenix’s independent specialists.
Whatever it is, we’ll fix it.

Phoenix European Motors is an independent shop and the perfect place to bring your car. We can handle any repair, from a simple brake pad or rotor change to a deep engine overhaul. We work on all European makes, including Jaguar, Land Rover, Range Rover, BMW, Mercedes-Benz, Audi, Porsche, Volkswagen, Volvo and more, with our most recent years focused on Jaguar, Land Rover, and Range Rover.

Our master technician brings over a decade of hands-on experience: ten years running his own shop in Europe, followed by time here working at an independent JLR specialist. That know-how goes into every car that comes through our doors.

We use OEM parts and proper diagnostic equipment, and we don’t cut corners. Every job starts with a free inspection and estimate, so you’ll know what needs attention and what it’ll cost before any work begins. Easy to reach from anywhere in the Valley.

Trust your car to someone who truly knows it. Schedule your visit today.
