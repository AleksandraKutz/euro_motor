ABOUT US

Family-owned. JLR specialists.

Phoenix European Motors is a small, family-run independent shop in Phoenix specializing in European automobiles. We work on all European makes, including Jaguar, Land Rover, Range Rover, BMW, Mercedes-Benz, Audi, Porsche, Volkswagen, Volvo and more — with our most recent years focused on Jaguar, Land Rover, and Range Rover. We use OEM parts and proper diagnostic equipment, and we don't cut corners. A free initial inspection and estimate is included — you'll know what needs attention and what it'll cost before any work begins. Conveniently located with easy access from across the Valley.
